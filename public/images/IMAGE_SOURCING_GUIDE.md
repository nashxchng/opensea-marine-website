# Image Sourcing Guide for Opensea Marine Website

## The Challenge

Finding accurate images for specialized maritime equipment (AHT vessels, PSV vessels, swamp rigs, land rigs, bunker operations) is **moderately difficult** because:

1. **Generic stock photos** don't match specific vessel types
2. **Unsplash/Pexels** have limited specialized maritime/oil & gas content
3. **Industry-specific imagery** requires specialized sources or actual operational photos

## Best Solutions (Ranked by Quality)

### 1. **Your Own Operational Photos** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (BEST)
- **Source**: Photos from your actual fleet and operations
- **Pros**: 100% accurate, authentic, builds trust
- **Cons**: Requires photography or access to operations
- **Action**: Contact operations team for vessel/rig photos

### 2. **Industry-Specific Stock Sites** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Sources**:
  - Getty Images (maritime/oil & gas collections)
  - Shutterstock (search: "AHT vessel", "platform supply vessel", "drilling rig")
  - Alamy (specialized maritime photography)
- **Pros**: More accurate than generic stock
- **Cons**: Requires licensing fees ($50-200 per image)

### 3. **Maritime Equipment Manufacturers** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Sources**: 
  - Vessel manufacturer websites (often have high-quality photos)
  - Rig manufacturer galleries
  - Equipment supplier catalogs
- **Pros**: Technically accurate
- **Cons**: May require permission for commercial use

### 4. **Industry Publications** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Sources**:
  - Offshore Engineer magazine
  - Maritime Reporter
  - Oil & Gas Journal
- **Pros**: Real operational photos
- **Cons**: Requires licensing agreements

### 5. **Generic Stock (Current Approach)** ⭐⭐
- **Sources**: Unsplash, Pexels
- **Pros**: Free, easy to use
- **Cons**: Often inaccurate, doesn't match descriptions

## Recommended Image Specifications

### AHT Vessels
- **What to look for**: Tug vessels with anchor handling winches, towing operations
- **Keywords**: "anchor handling tug", "AHT vessel", "offshore tug", "towing vessel"
- **Alternative**: Generic tug boats (less accurate but better than nothing)

### PSV Vessels
- **What to look for**: Supply vessels with cargo decks, at offshore platforms
- **Keywords**: "platform supply vessel", "PSV", "offshore supply vessel", "cargo vessel"
- **Alternative**: General cargo ships (less accurate)

### Swamp Rigs
- **What to look for**: Drilling rigs in wetland/shallow water environments
- **Keywords**: "swamp rig", "wetland drilling", "shallow water rig", "barge rig"
- **Alternative**: General drilling rigs (less accurate)

### Land Rigs
- **What to look for**: Onshore drilling rigs with derricks
- **Keywords**: "land drilling rig", "onshore rig", "drilling derrick"
- **Alternative**: General oil rigs (more accurate than swamp rigs)

### Bunker Trading
- **What to look for**: Fuel barges, bunkering operations, vessels refueling
- **Keywords**: "bunker barge", "marine fuel", "vessel refueling", "bunkering"
- **Alternative**: General tanker ships (less accurate)
